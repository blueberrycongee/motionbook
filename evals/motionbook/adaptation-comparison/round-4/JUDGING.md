# Blind review contract

Review the rendered A–F artifacts and actual browser recordings against the supplied briefs and common reference images. Do not read the condition map, skill variants, implementation reports, prior scores, or other judges' conclusions before freezing your report.

For each task, give a preference order or a genuine tie with concrete observed reasons. Separately record:
- Layout and hierarchy: alignment, density, reading width, component placement, narrow-layout behavior.
- Visual treatment: typography, contrast, corner relationships, surfaces and shadows; distinguish taste from a visible defect.
- Motion: continuity and interruption in the recording; never infer real-time smoothness from stills alone.
- Interaction: observed input/focus/navigation behavior; distinguish a screenshot from a passing browser check.

Do not reward more decoration, extra instructions, or a longer implementation. Judge fitness for the brief rather than literal screenshot copying. A tie is valid; do not invent a numerical margin or general claim from one sample. List unavailable evidence. Freeze your independent report before learning the condition assignments.
