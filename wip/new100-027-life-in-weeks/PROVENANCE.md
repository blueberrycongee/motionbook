# Provenance and checkpoint status

Independent reconstruction of [Life in Weeks](https://www.inspora.design/posts/8-9), attributed to [@jeetnirnejak](https://x.com/jeetnirnejak/status/2088251238530105638).

Original reference: https://media.inspora.design/posts/f50d7b71-f991-44d2-849c-c3f5e583cb05.mp4 . SHA-256 b073e02bef5203ea4a4e64ccbef6ca0f4f2f716c1d53b846be908f6000dd506e. The 1920 × 1514 video has 445 native 30 fps frames, PTS 0–14.8 seconds. Original footage and extracted images remain local analysis inputs and are not part of the package.

All UI geometry is drawn independently in SVG. Counts were measured from the source's visible counters at every native frame and reconciled with the 4,680-week total; seven ambiguous OCR readings were inspected directly. The original 026/027 local files were unavailable after recovery. This package is a new implementation; historical reviews do not validate it.

The first source frame and 20 overview frames were inspected during this rebuild. Complete every-frame visual pairing and independent final review remain pending. This checkpoint has no completed motion export and does not count as a finished example. Node checks cover only current geometry and model data; browser runtime has not been exercised. Font substitutes are licensed Inter and Geist Mono; notices are in licenses/. The source animation's font and footage license have not been independently established.
