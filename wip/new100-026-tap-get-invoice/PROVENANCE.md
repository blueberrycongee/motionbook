# Provenance

Independent reconstruction of the public [Tap / get invoice study](https://www.inspora.design/posts/tap-get-invoice), linked by the curator to [@movindesign_ on X](https://x.com/movindesign_/status/2101688131854483939). The curator's creator field also named @ankur5ahu; original authorship is not independently resolved.

Observed original video: https://media.inspora.design/posts/f590c603-6748-410b-b725-983efa9731b8.mp4 . SHA-256 b61bd9fd7dc845eeddd3d05441f977d25d64836368ba9b2730858a632780009c. The 824 × 720 original contains 831 encoded frames; their native PTS span 0 to 13.833437 seconds. It was fetched anew for this reconstruction. Footage and decoded images remain local analysis inputs and are not included here.

Scene geometry, glyph placement, paper homographies, icons, decorative line pattern and code are authored independently from visual observation and measured scalar landmarks. No original screenshots, video pixels, proprietary project files or UI artwork are embedded. The source's display text is reproduced as fictional demonstration UI; it is not a transaction or working invoice service.

Inter font contours are from the project's previously verified licensed font subset. The serif subset was generated from installed Liberation Serif. Their redistribution notices are in licenses/. Source footage rights were not specified, so no license is asserted over the original design or footage.

This is a new reconstruction, not restoration of the unavailable old 026 freeze. Earlier historical validation does not validate this code.
