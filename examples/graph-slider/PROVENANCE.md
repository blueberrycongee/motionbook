# Source and rights

Reference: [Graph Slider](https://rauno.me/craft/graph-slider) by [Rauno Freiberg](https://x.com/raunofreiberg). The author credits Family's iOS app for the idea and Siddhartha for the graph.

The graph, marker, guide, gradients, tooltip and pointer are independently drawn. No original code, footage or source pixels are bundled. Pointer movement maps to distance along the curve while the tooltip keeps a fixed vertical position.
