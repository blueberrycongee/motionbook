Study Sans Regular and Semibold are static instances of the Inter variable font by The Inter Project Authors. Instances use optical size14 and weights400/600. They are renamed to honor reserved font names and remain under SIL Open Font License1.1. Full original copyright/license: Inter-LICENSE.txt. No font license is applied to the independent application code.

StudyHand-Regular.ttf and StudyHand-Semibold.ttf are static weight 400/600 instances of Caveat, renamed Study Hand. The upstream SIL OFL is retained in Caveat-OFL.txt.

StudyHand-Bold.ttf is a renamed static weight700 Caveat instance under the same SIL OFL. Mono-Bold.ttf is DejaVu Sans Mono Bold, covered by FONT-LICENSE.txt.
