# Motion and visual limits

The replay preserves the eight-swipe sequence, including the reverse return and repeated forward swipe. Camera motion and internal scroll are independent; the pin stays fixed. Incoming writing, outgoing paper depth, counter rolls, colored underlines and the three strike-throughs use separate timing.

An authored return restores the first note for replay. Touch indicators are omitted. Paper, shadows and icons are independently drawn, so photographic grain, lighting and glyph contours differ.
