# Visual self-review

Source: Rehan Ahmed’s Sticky-note app, original 2000×2000 video, 383 encoded frames, 16 seconds. The original presentation timestamps were used with zero time offset.

Every original source/replica pair was visually inspected across 22 dense contact pages. After the independent review’s ink and decoration corrections, all 383 pairs were viewed again on the final bound sequence. Full-resolution follow-ups checked clipped lettering, counter rolls, released-paper depth and the final strike-throughs. All 72 frames of the authored closing return were also inspected. `REVIEW-BINDING.json` binds this review to the final source, scene and media hashes.

## Checked and corrected

- Original eight-swipe sequence, including the reverse return from Physics lab to Study group and the repeated forward swipe.
- Camera enlargement and retreat; internal scroll independent of the camera; fixed pushpin; clipped physical paper corners and relaxing outgoing angles.
- Delayed appearance of incoming writing, retained outgoing lettering, and the change of paper depth after release.
- Thin trailing sheets, independently drawn gradients and cast shadows, body typography and locally bundled static font weights.
- Counter rolls at their observed times rather than at dominant-paper color crossings.
- Standup’s three sequential drawn strike-throughs; all six active-note colored time underlines and their measured draw-on phases; the partial white lower panel.
- Incoming ink-opacity envelopes, the faint reverse Physics remainder, and all footer-link underlines.
- Authored return to the first note and identical first/last raw scene pixels.

## Scope and limits

The device, paper, shadows and icons are independently drawn. Source recording touch indicators are omitted. Fine photographic grain, material lighting and renderer-dependent glyph contours differ slightly; no pixel-perfect or numeric imitation-percentage claim is made.

Actual browser runtime was not executed. This is an offline visual self-review, separate from state and simulated DOM-event tests. Independent publication review remains a separate gate.
