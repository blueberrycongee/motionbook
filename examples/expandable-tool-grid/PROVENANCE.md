## Motion

The bar pinches before expanding into three rows. The plus rotates to × while its surface brightens; content blurs out before the pinch and returns within the new bounds. A separate selection lens bends only icon strokes crossing its optical boundary.

The replay includes interrupted open/close/open movement and press flashes. An authored 8.24–8.7-second tail restores the initial tab highlight for looping.
