## Observed motion

The pill-shaped four-item bar pinches narrower before expanding upward into a three-row editing-tool grid. The plus rotates into an × while its circular surface brightens and changes size. Content blurs away before the pinch, then reappears inside the new bounds.

The complete original timeline is retained, including the initial selected-tab movement, repeated expansion and collapse, the interrupted open/close/open sequence around frames 209–246, Undo and Duplicate press flashes, and the final close. The container trace was measured at multiple luminance thresholds; it represents visible bounds including antialiasing, not recovered original CSS. The toggle angle was estimated from the fourth moment of visible plus/X strokes. Saturated low-contrast frames are interpolated rather than claimed as directly measurable.

The first 495 exported states use the source's actual frame PTS. They are encoded at nominal 60 fps; source timestamp irregularities are below one millisecond. An explicitly authored 8.24–8.7 s tail restores the initial tab highlight and geometry for a seamless repeating preview. It is outside the original recording.

Comparison images remain local analysis material. The runnable demo and previews contain only the independent implementation.

The initial tab-selection lens is a separate overlay. Its visible boundary was measured separately from the bar itself, because a single connected-component measurement includes the lens overshoot. An inverse SVG displacement filter bends only the icon strokes crossing the moving lens boundary. No original icon pixels are sampled.
