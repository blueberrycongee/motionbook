# Provenance and scope

An independent study of OpenAI's PiP interaction contracts. The menu, mascot and hover-control icons are original drawings; see [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md) and [asset attribution](../public/assets/ATTRIBUTION.txt). No reference footage, source screenshots, proprietary bundles or extracted icons are included.

The previews are offline SVG/Sharp renders. The snap solver runs at 60 Hz and is sampled at 30 fps: slow drag, fast flick and a half-speed replay of the fast release. The hover preview illustrates scripted states rather than measured native trajectories.

The demo uses local fixtures. Private backdrop filters are approximated; remote transport and real IAB/Chrome focus handoff are not implemented.
