# Source and authorship

Reference: [Task Card](https://www.inspora.design/posts/task-card), credited to [Adrian Kuleszo](https://x.com/adriankuleszo/status/2094402386383208575), @adriankuleszo.

The original public footage is https://media.inspora.design/posts/064cccc0-d6e2-42b0-bcd7-26d3722ed6e2.mp4. The observed original is 960 × 720, 348 decoded frames at 60 fps, 5.8 seconds. SHA-256: `d96fbec861dfbfd7713513b8cad3cd75384a3598ba50dc93813ef1dfc8b16acf`. Analysis used the original native frames and PTS, not a lower-rate preview. Reference footage and comparison crops are kept outside this package.

No redistribution license for the reference footage was established. This implementation includes no source frames, screenshots, texture samples or copied video. Device, card surfaces, shadows, symbols, gesture capsules and completion state are authored SVG geometry. The small Slack symbol is an independently drawn identifying mark shown in the reference; no endorsement or trademark rights are implied.

Motion data describes observed native timing, visible phone bounds and card edge/promotion trajectories. These are appearance measurements, not recovered CSS or original application code. Obscured card transforms and a short clean-loop tail are independently fitted. Typographic line widths are measured appearance fits to licensed substitute glyphs; the source font itself was not established.

Inter Regular, Medium and SemiBold are unmodified font binaries obtained from the official Inter distribution, https://rsms.me/inter/ and its `font-files/Inter-{Regular,Medium,SemiBold}.woff2` files, with corresponding TrueType copies used by the offline renderer. The original SIL Open Font License is preserved in `assets/Inter-LICENSE.txt`.

Liberation Serif Regular is the unmodified installed Liberation font, copied from `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/liberation/LiberationSerif-Regular.ttf`. Its existing SIL Open Font License and upstream notices are preserved in `assets/Liberation-LICENSE.txt`; project: https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts.
