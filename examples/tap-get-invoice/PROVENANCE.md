# Provenance

Independent reconstruction of the public [Tap / get invoice study](https://www.inspora.design/posts/tap-get-invoice), linked by the curator to [@movindesign_ on X](https://x.com/movindesign_/status/2101688131854483939). The curator's creator field also named @ankur5ahu; original authorship is not independently resolved.

Observed original video: https://media.inspora.design/posts/f590c603-6748-410b-b725-983efa9731b8.mp4 . SHA-256 b61bd9fd7dc845eeddd3d05441f977d25d64836368ba9b2730858a632780009c. The 824 × 720 original has 831 encoded video frames with native PTS 0–13.833437 seconds; the video stream duration is 13.850156 seconds, and the full container is 13.888 seconds. This is the original post media, not the gallery preview. It was fetched and inspected anew on 2026-10-06.

The full membership screen is recreated: seven icon rows, the lower patterned card, paper content and colored geometric mark, the sequential upper/lower folds, separate returning lip and backing, pen strokes, and recorded pointer states. The three opening cycles and both closing cycles are preserved. The third cycle ends open in the source; a hold and crossfade reset extend the replica to 15.6 seconds for looping.

All scene geometry, paper homographies, icons, decorative line segments, pointer shapes and cubic pen paths are authored independently from visual observation and scalar landmarks. Pen prefix timing is fitted to observed stroke appearance. No original screenshots, video pixels, proprietary project files or original UI artwork are embedded. Original footage and decoded comparison images remain outside the published payload. The displayed invoice text is reproduced as demonstration UI; it is not a working payment service.

Inter font contours come from the project's verified licensed subset. The serif subset was generated from Liberation Serif. The outline representation is named Invoice Study Glyphs; complete font notices are retained in licenses/. The source animation's exact fonts and footage license were not independently established. No license is asserted over the original design or footage.

This package was rebuilt after the earlier local work became unavailable. Earlier validation of the missing historical 026 package does not validate this reconstruction; fresh checks and review records are included in validation/.

The final optical correction uses Medium outlines for the two item amounts. It changes no paper, pen, pointer or control timing. The final delta and its pixel scope are recorded in validation/change-proof.json.
