# Provenance and asset boundaries

- Reconstruction source: the independent Mac Studio seven-study package, version 0.1.2, study `06-parallax`; integrated under `mac-studio-parallax-features` on 2026-10-07
- Reference: [Apple Mac Studio](https://www.apple.com/mac-studio/) and public [page script](https://www.apple.com/v/mac-studio/o/built/scripts/main.built.js), inspected during the original study on 2026-10-07. Mechanism details and deliberate differences are recorded in this example's README
- Browser source, procedural hardware, artwork and independent preview are reconstruction work. Apple photographs, original JPEG frame sequences, Apple videos/logos and official reference captures are not bundled here
- Bundled Liberation Sans font files, where present, retain their actual license in `assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. No new blanket license is inferred for reference material or third-party assets
- `preview/loop.gif` is a compressed 720×491 web preview of `06-parallax-recreated.gif`. All 120 frames, their individual delays, the 8.05-second duration and infinite looping are retained. Downscaling and 24-color palette reduction change displayed pixels; this preview is not lossless or pixel-identical to the original export. It remains a normal-speed offline recreation, not an official comparison or browser recording
- The 03 benchmark values are fictional. The 02 dimensions are illustrative. Nothing here represents verified product performance
- The original high-resolution GIF is retained separately and is not duplicated in this package. [Compression evidence](../../integration-tests/mac-studio/qa/parallax-web-preview-compression.json) records both encoded hashes, dimensions and the complete frame timing. The animation and rendering source remain unchanged
