MOTION STUDY 06

A single image. Different velocities.

One original back-panel illustration scales from 1.4 to 1. The headline travels upward by half a viewport while the image remains slower. A quiet white copy block takes over at the end.

Reduced motion is enabled. This scene stays on its complete, readable composition. You can still inspect individual moments with the seek control.

How the scene unfolds

  1. Image layer: a single deterministic illustration scales from 1.4 to 1
  2. Headline layer: an independent ease-in-out track travels up 50vh
  3. Content layer: copy fades across a quarter-viewport scroll interval
  4. Exit: the picture leaves and the complete copy stays readable

Controls

Scroll down or back up to scrub. Drag the timeline or use its arrow keys for precise seeking. Play runs an illustrative seven-second pass; Repeat loops it. Reset returns to the beginning. The duration describes this demo, not the source website.

About this study

The motion mechanism is informed by the Mac Studio page. All hardware, chips, and abstract graphics here are drawn from scratch in Canvas 2D. Specifications are fictional visual-demo content. No Apple product imagery, logos, sequence frames, or fonts are included.
