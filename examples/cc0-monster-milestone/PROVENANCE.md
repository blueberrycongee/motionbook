## Motion study

The earlier study measured the 7.50-second rhythm of the official Duolingo 2022 streak animation:

- https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/
- https://blog.duolingo.com/streak-milestone-design-animation/

This revision keeps only broad phase timing: count descent, anticipation, upright spin, burst, landing and delayed controls. It changes character anatomy, silhouette, palette, celebration imagery, text and interface. It is not presented as a source-fidelity recreation of that product. No Duolingo artwork, frames, logo, original GIF, bird rig, fire form, wings or sunglasses is bundled or used by the revised page.
