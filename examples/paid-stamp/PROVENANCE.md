Observed 2026-10-05–06. All 523 original frames were inspected chronologically. The payment-day wheel changes 13→14→15, the surrounding form fades, the picker becomes an extruded white stamp, and a perspective invoice is revealed. The stamp descends, prints the red date, rebounds, and exposes an Undo/Done confirmation. Done dismisses the physical stamp and returns the completed document to a flat view with download/next controls.

The panel, projected paper, stamp body and sole, cat symbol, printed border, touch rings and shadows are independently authored vector geometry and analytic gradients. Native scalar poses, colors, timing and text landmarks were measured from the footage. Source pixels and source-only measurement templates remain outside this package; no reference image, video, original code or extracted graphic is embedded or played back.

Glyph outlines use Inter Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold under the [SIL Open Font License](licenses/Inter-LICENSE.txt). The printed date uses independently adjusted Inter glyph proportions, spacing and optical weight; no proprietary font was recovered. The original typeface was not established; this is a licensed substitute. It is not extracted from the reference. All paper content is laid out anew, including the illustrative business/bank text visible in the footage. The demo has no payment or upload functionality.

The original visual sequence is retained, followed by a short authored crossfade/reset for a clean loop. Drag, wheel and keyboard date input, extra status/color choices, undo, next-invoice and reduced-motion policies are authored demo behavior beyond what the recording establishes. The local PDF writer creates an illustrative PDF in the browser without contacting a service; it is not a payment request.
