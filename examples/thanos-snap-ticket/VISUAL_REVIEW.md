# Visual limitations

Train, landscape and phone are independently drawn vectors; blossoms, carriage detail and reflections differ. The QR pattern and particle seed are independent. Particle direction, density and timing follow the reference, but individual dots do not. Reverse QR wipe timing is separate from the forward summary wipe and from returning text particles.

Inter substitutes for the unidentified original typeface. Recorded touch indicators are omitted. Offline frame comparisons do not verify browser behavior.
