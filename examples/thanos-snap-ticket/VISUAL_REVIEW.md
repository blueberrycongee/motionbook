## Fidelity limits

The train scene is a close independent vector reconstruction, not the source illustration. Small blossom contours, carriage surface detail and phone reflections therefore differ. The QR-like pattern is deliberately independent. Particle positions use an independent deterministic seed; front progression, direction, density and overall timing are fitted to the reference, rather than claiming identical random dots. Inter substitutes for the source's unprovided typeface. Recorded touch indicators are omitted from the clean demo.

The complete original presentation, not just the dissolving card, is the comparison target. Source footage and source-pixel comparison sheets are retained separately for private review and are not distributed with the package. Automated tests do not establish visual fidelity.

The last reverse-pass revision slows the QR wipe independently from the forward summary wipe and adjusts returning text-particle density, offsets and lifetime. Its 60 changed native SVGs were isolated by exact comparison against the prior fully reviewed scene. All 64 enclosing native-frame pairs were re-inspected; the other 211 source-native SVGs are unchanged.

## Focused v3 correction

The preserved v2 failed independent review because reverse frames 118–127 and 238–247 retained an overly rectangular, dense QR cloud, with the later trail sitting too low. The separate v3 changes only the reverse wipe and particle model. The QR area spreads more broadly, rises and drifts right; its density tapers independently from the smaller text particles. All 60 changed native SVGs fall inside the two reverse windows. Exact comparisons prove that the remaining 211 native SVGs are unchanged. Both the implementation worker and publisher inspected every one of the 64 enclosing source/replica pairs in the final v16 comparison set.
