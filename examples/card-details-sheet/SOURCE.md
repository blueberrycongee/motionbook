# Reference

Card details sheet by @bodya_zhk: [original post](https://x.com/bodya_zhk/status/2090854247604638145), [Inspora](https://www.inspora.design/posts/1-34).

The phone dips, the card drops and a highlight crosses its lettering. A details sheet rises over independently blurred and dimmed dashboard layers; dismissal restores the dashboard and rolls the balance digits back into place.

`motion-data.js` contains numeric phone, card, sheet and dimming tracks; `scene.js` draws the interface. No account, payment or network operation occurs. See [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).
