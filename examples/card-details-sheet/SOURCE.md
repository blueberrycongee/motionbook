A bank-card dashboard dips with the phone while its silver card drops farther within the screen. A diagonal highlight travels across the card lettering. The balance blurs before the rest of the dashboard. The phone returns as a dark details sheet rises, and separate blur and dimming tracks soften the dashboard behind it. On dismissal, the sheet drops with a brief fade and blur. The background clears and individual balance digits roll back to their settled values.

The replica retains the complete square reference presentation, including the large phone extending beyond the lower edge. `motion-data.js` contains independently measured numerical tracks for the phone, card, sheet and dimming. It contains no source pixels. The entire native sequence, frames 0–120, is used at its original presentation timestamps.

The device, card, small portrait, icons, wordmarks and controller are independently drawn or written. Names, card number and clipboard values are fictional samples. No account, payment or network operation occurs.
