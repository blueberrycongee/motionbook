## Actual-footage observation

A 5 fps contact sheet and full-resolution idle frame were inspected. Source timing is retained in the complete 6.6-second reconstruction:

- 0.0–1.27 s: agreement details, blue gradient, Cancel and Sign pills.
- 1.27–1.47 s: Sign text/icon fade; 1.43–1.88 s: a small handwritten signature is progressively drawn inside the white pill.
- 1.9–2.94 s: completed signature holds without changing the document body.
- 2.94–3.32 s: document details blur and fade; a second paper/check icon and two-line confirmation move gently into the center. Button changes to Back to Docs.
- 3.32–5.48 s: successful-signature state holds.
- 5.48–5.76 s: the details and initial Sign button return. The final 0.84 s matches the starting state and forms the clean loop.

The reference remains local analysis material and is not inside this package.
