## Native-footage observations

All 393 native source frames were paired with decoded replica frames and inspected. Full-resolution phase and transition details supplement the complete contact-sheet review.

- 0–1.14 s: agreement details and two action pills hold.
- 1.14–1.26 s: the Sign label fades. The native signature reveal is a horizontal wipe over a small handwritten vector shape, beginning around 1.45 s and completing around 1.94 s. It is not a simple stroke-dash draw.
- 1.94–2.92 s: the signature holds.
- Around 2.95 s: the signature label leaves upward. Sharp and defocused document-body layers crossfade while the paper/check and confirmation appear; Back to Docs enters from below. The body transition is separate from the confirmation opacity.
- The success state holds, then reverses from about 5.43 s. Back to Docs moves down and Sign returns from above.
- The final restored hold closes the 6.55-second loop.

Reference images used for comparison remain local analysis material and are excluded from this package. Numeric per-frame diagnostics and final artifact hashes are in audit/.
