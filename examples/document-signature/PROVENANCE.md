## Motion

The 6.55-second sequence holds agreement details until 1.14 seconds, fades Sign at 1.14–1.26, and reveals the signature with a horizontal wipe at about 1.45–1.94. Around 2.95, the signature leaves upward while sharp/defocused document layers crossfade independently of the confirmation. The return begins around 5.43 seconds: Back to Docs moves down and Sign returns from above.
