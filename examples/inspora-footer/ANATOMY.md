# Horse silhouette

The original SVG illustration uses broad shoulder/thigh mass, tapered forearms and gaskins, narrow cannons, articulated joints, a short pastern and wedge-shaped hoof. These are art-direction choices in artwork coordinates:

- Upper attachments: fore 15.6, hind 18.5 units
- Proximal forearm/gaskin: 11.2 / 11.8 units, tapering toward the joint
- Cannon top: 4.8 units; hoof base: 9.4 units
- A compact fore carpus, backward-projecting hock and rear-projecting fetlock clarify the joints
- Hatching emphasizes upper muscle mass; lighter distal highlights keep the legs readable

## Shape references

- [University of Minnesota, Conformation of the horse](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/animals-and-livestock/horse/conformation-of-the-horse): muscular upper limbs and narrower cannons. Copyrighted photograph used only for study, not bundled.
- [J. Wortley Axe, Exterior of the Horse — Side View (1905)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Exterior_of_the_Horse_-_Side_View.jpg): engraved contours, hock, fetlock and hoof. Commons marks it public domain in the United States.
- [University of Arkansas, Horse Conformation Analysis](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/publications/pdf/FSA3029_2020.pdf): shoulder, forearm and gaskin mass.
