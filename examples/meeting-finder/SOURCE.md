# Reference

Meeting Finder by @jeetnirnejak: [original post](https://x.com/jeetnirnejak/status/2084627211257339954), [Inspora](https://www.inspora.design/posts/1-44).

Scrubbing updates the chosen UTC hour immediately while the tall outlined cursor follows with spring-like motion. Clocks and day indicators update; at 16:00 UTC all four cities overlap. Find best time performs a stepped search and fading wave through the grid.

`motion-data.js` stores cursor, selected-hour and cap-color tracks; `pulse-data.js` stores cell opacity. The replay adds an authored return to 05:00 after 14.85 seconds, closing at 16.8 seconds. No source pixels are included; see [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).
