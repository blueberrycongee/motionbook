The effect is Sketcha's black-cat entry: a seated cat glances over its shoulder, hops and turns face-forward, splays four paws, scratches down the cyan surface, and tears it open to a pastel menu. It is not a generic onboarding carousel or a full somersault.

## Fonts
The precise original system font cannot be established from a filmed handset. The implementation uses bundled, renamed subsets of Liberation Sans and Noto Sans CJK SC as stable substitutes. Font license notices are in `assets/fonts/`.

- Liberation Fonts: https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts — SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Noto CJK: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk — SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Subset family names: Onboarding Sans and Onboarding Chinese. Runtime CSS aliases: Study Sans and Study CJK.

## Scope
The supplied interaction reproduces the complete sequence actually visible in the reference, from cat activation to the menu. Menu cards support press feedback and accessible labels. A drawing editor, real camera, photo library, history database, and settings screens are not depicted by the reference and are not implemented. Nothing requests device permissions, uploads data, signs in, or makes network requests.
