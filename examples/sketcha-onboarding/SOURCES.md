## Fonts

Bundled, renamed subsets substitute for the unidentified original font. Notices are retained in `assets/fonts/`:

- [Liberation Fonts](https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts), SIL OFL 1.1; subset family Onboarding Sans, CSS alias Study Sans.
- [Noto CJK](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk), SIL OFL 1.1; subset family Onboarding Chinese, CSS alias Study CJK.

Menu cards provide press feedback and accessible labels only. Drawing, camera, photo-library, history and settings features are not implemented. The demo makes no network or permission requests.
