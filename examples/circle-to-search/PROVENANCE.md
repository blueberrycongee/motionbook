# Provenance

Independent Circle to Search interaction study using original vector illustration and a local sample catalog. No recognition service, uploads, accounts or Google search API.

Default appearance: [Google February 2026 demonstration](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/circle-to-search-february-2026/), cross-checked against the [June 2026 filmed demonstration](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/android-drop-june-2026/). Historical mode: [January 2024 demonstration](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/google-circle-to-search-android/). These references do not establish one universal rendering across current devices/builds.

Official videos, photographs, decoded source frames and source-pixel comparison media are excluded. Fonts retain the included Inter SIL OFL license. Google and Circle to Search marks belong to their owners; this is an independent study, not an official component.
