# Provenance

Independent reconstruction of [Life in Weeks](https://www.inspora.design/posts/8-9), attributed by the curator to [@jeetnirnejak](https://x.com/jeetnirnejak/status/2088251238530105638).

Observed original media: https://media.inspora.design/posts/f50d7b71-f991-44d2-849c-c3f5e583cb05.mp4 . SHA-256 b073e02bef5203ea4a4e64ccbef6ca0f4f2f716c1d53b846be908f6000dd506e. The 1920 × 1514 original contains 445 encoded video frames at 30 fps, with native PTS 0–14.8 seconds and a video duration of 14.833333 seconds. This is the original post media, not the gallery preview. It was fetched and inspected anew during this recovery implementation.

All card, grid, slider, pointer and marker geometry is drawn independently in SVG. Each of the 4,680 cells remains editable geometry. Native counter values, row-front positions/fade widths, marker core and pulse dimensions, age-pill position/ink, sparse highlighted-cell opacities and pointer bounds were measured from the visible footage. The main boundary and isolated highlights are modeled separately. These scalar observations do not identify the original application's implementation. No original screenshots, video pixels, project files or proprietary artwork are embedded in the demo.

The full card, stage legend, three counters, age control and pointer sequence are retained. The current-cell marker has its own observed trajectory and can lag the displayed counter. A final hold, crossfade reset and opening hold extend the source sequence to 16.8 seconds. This closure and the freely interactive slider response are authored additions.

Inter and Geist Mono subset outlines are distributed under their SIL Open Font Licenses. The outline representation is named Life Study Glyphs; complete notices are in licenses/. The source animation's exact fonts and footage license have not been independently established. No license is asserted over the original design or footage. Original footage and decoded comparison images remain local analysis inputs and are excluded from the published payload.

This is a new reconstruction after the earlier local files became unavailable. Historical reviews of the missing implementation do not validate this package. Fresh visual coverage, tests, raster bindings and remaining differences are documented separately in VALIDATION.md and validation/.
