# Provenance

Independent recreation of Telegram’s [December 2022 Hidden Media demonstration](https://telegram.org/blog/hidden-media-zero-storage-profile-pics#hidden-media). See [numeric source measurements](evidence/source-measurements.md) and [public release provenance](evidence/public-release.json).

Artwork and chat copy are original. No Telegram account, client code, official photo, source video or source-pixel comparison media is packaged. Telegram names and source rights remain with their respective owners. This is not an official component.

The 180 ms reveal envelope is calibrated to the second reveal in the historic promotion. It does not establish exact native timing across current builds; particle trajectories and shader parameters are independently approximated.
