# Reference and rights

An unofficial study of Apple's Genie window-to-Dock effect, with independent code and artwork. No Apple code, wallpaper, icons, fonts, logos or screenshots are used by the app. Reference footage and comparison imagery are not bundled.

Motion references are attributed in [FIDELITY.md](FIDELITY.md). Historical context: [Apple Dock settings](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/deployment/depef1fdf19/web), [US7362331B2, Bas Ording](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7362331B2/en), [Ording's interview](https://archive.computerhistory.org/resources/access/text/2018/10/102738558-05-01-acc.pdf) and [Dock engineer James Thomson's account](https://tla.systems/blog/2025/01/04/i-live-my-life-a-quarter-century-at-a-time/).
