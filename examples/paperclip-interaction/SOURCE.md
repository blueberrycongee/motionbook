A +0.050 s offset into the preview-clock model aligns it with the original presentation clock; this is explicitly recorded in review evidence.

## Behavior

Share opens the panel. A paper card resolves from a blurred transcript. The metal clip rises from behind the paper, clears its edge, falls onto the front, and settles as rear sheets spread. Hovering the clip collapses those sheets; leaving it restores them. The original repeats this hover interaction twice.

## Construction

All paper shapes, metal strokes, shadows, brand-icon approximations, and UI are authored SVG. No source imagery or source code is embedded. Headline opacity and blur were fitted from original-frame intensity and edge measurements; clip travel was fitted from observed wire bounds. The body paragraph is independently worded.

The clean loop preserves the source opening and both hover cycles, then adds a closing fade and rest so its boundary is continuous. No instruction labels or comparison overlays are drawn in the demo.
