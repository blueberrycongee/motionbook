## Observed timing

The full 303-frame recording is retained: empty startup, warm brightness fill and staggered 52% entry, the mode change to volume with blue fill and 53%, clockwise dial adjustment to 72%, counterclockwise reduction to 14%, and the final display fade while Volume mode remains selected.

Digits move independently. The rise enters from below; the fall enters from above. The percent sign has a longer, separate settling interval. Numeric position/luminance observations are visual measurements, not recovered CSS values. Repeating dial ticks make fast absolute rotation ambiguous; the authored rotation curve follows the observable direction and slowdown without claiming a uniquely recovered angle.

An additional 10.1–10.7-second loop tail resets the mode and title after the original recording ends. It is explicitly authored, not part of the source.
