# Provenance and asset boundaries

- Reconstruction source: the independent Mac Studio seven-study package, version 0.1.2, study `03-stats`; integrated under `mac-studio-benchmark-tabs` on 2026-10-07
- Reference: [Apple Mac Studio](https://www.apple.com/mac-studio/) and public [page script](https://www.apple.com/v/mac-studio/o/built/scripts/main.built.js), inspected during the original study on 2026-10-07. Mechanism details and deliberate differences are recorded in this example's README
- Browser source, procedural hardware, artwork and independent preview are reconstruction work. Apple photographs, original JPEG frame sequences, Apple videos/logos and official reference captures are not bundled here
- Bundled Liberation Sans font files, where present, retain their actual license in `assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. No new blanket license is inferred for reference material or third-party assets
- `preview/loop.gif` is losslessly re-encoded from `03-stats-recreated.gif`, preserving every displayed RGBA frame, frame delay, loop count and dimension; it is the normal-speed offline recreation, not an official comparison or browser recording
- The 03 benchmark values are fictional. The 02 dimensions are illustrative. Nothing here represents verified product performance
- Encoding-only hashes and full frame/timing equivalence are recorded in [the media equivalence proof](../../integration-tests/mac-studio/qa/gif-encoding-equivalence.json)
