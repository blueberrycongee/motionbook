MOTION STUDY 05

Six scenes. One continuous gesture.

A neutral studio computer rotates from front to back, fills the frame, and hands off to a woven light environment. A black performance scene resolves into two original chips and five staggered specifications.

Reduced motion is enabled. This scene stays on its complete, readable composition. You can still inspect individual moments with the seek control.

How the scene unfolds

  1. Front: a calm product view and centered title
  2. Turn: the same hardware rotates to its rear panel
  3. Push in: the camera advances through the vent field
  4. Color environment: an original woven light sculpture fills the stage
  5. Performance: the image falls to black and a new heading rises
  6. Chips: two cards arrive from opposite sides, then five specs stagger

Original demo specifications

N1 Core: 12-core compute, 32-core graphics, 96 GB memory, and 600 GB/s bandwidth. N2 Ultra: 24-core compute, 64-core graphics, 192 GB memory, and 1.2 TB/s bandwidth. These are fictional specifications for the motion study.

Controls

Scroll down or back up to scrub. Drag the timeline or use its arrow keys for precise seeking. Play runs an illustrative seven-second pass; Repeat loops it. Reset returns to the beginning. The duration describes this demo, not the source website.

About this study

The motion mechanism is informed by the Mac Studio page. All hardware, chips, and abstract graphics here are drawn from scratch in Canvas 2D. Specifications are fictional visual-demo content. No Apple product imagery, logos, sequence frames, or fonts are included.
