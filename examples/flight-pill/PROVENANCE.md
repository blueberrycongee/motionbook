## Motion

The pill contracts around 2.15–2.22 seconds and expands at 2.233–2.667. Metadata, airports, route, status, close control and counter reveal with separate blur delays. The airplane and route advance together while landing digits roll independently. Counter/route reverse near 5.0 seconds; the surface begins collapsing at 5.05 and settles into its starting geometry.
